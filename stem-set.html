Branding & Product Design

STEM SET

A portable physics toolbox that makes hands-on learning accessible anywhere.

Role

Product & Brand Designer

Timeline

6 Weeks

Tools

Figma, Illustrator

The Challenge

Science education often feels abstract to students when confined to textbooks. While laboratory equipment exists, it's typically bulky, fragile, expensive, and restricted to the classroom.

We needed to design the branding and digital companion experience for "STEM SET"—a physical, portable physics toolbox that allows students to perform real-world experiments safely at home.

[Placeholder Image: Research & Concept]
[Placeholder Image: Brand Identity]

The Solution

I developed a vibrant, approachable visual identity that strips away the intimidating "lab coat" aesthetic of traditional science kits, replacing it with bright, engaging colors and playful typography.

Alongside the physical packaging design, I architected a web app that guides students step-by-step through experiments using gamified 3D animations and interactive progress tracking.

Core Pillars

Visual Identity

A modern, geometric logo and brand system that appeals to both middle-school students and their parents, balancing fun with educational credibility.

Packaging Architecture

Designed the unboxing experience to be intuitive, with custom inserts that ensure fragile instruments are protected and easily identifiable.

Digital Companion App

A responsive web interface where students scan QR codes on their physical tools to instantly pull up video tutorials and interactive worksheets.

Gamified Learning

Incorporated a badge system that rewards students for completing complex physics modules, encouraging continuous engagement.

Outcome & Learnings

STEM SET required me to think beyond the screen. Designing for a physical-digital hybrid product taught me how deeply hardware constraints influence software design.

The resulting brand has successfully bridged the gap between play and education, proving that complex subjects don't have to look boring to be taken seriously.
