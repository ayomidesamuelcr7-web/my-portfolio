Branding & Product Design

STEM SET

A portable physics toolbox that makes hands-on learning accessible anywhere.

Role

Product & Brand Designer

Timeline

2 Days

Tools

Figma

The Challenge

Physics education often relies heavily on classrooms, laboratories, and specialized equipment. This can make hands-on experimentation difficult when students don't have access to the necessary resources outside a traditional learning environment.

Students need a way to learn physics concepts through practical experimentation without being limited by location or access to a fully equipped laboratory.

The Solution

STEM SET is a portable physics learning kit designed to make hands-on experimentation more accessible.

The kit brings essential tools and experiments together in a compact, organized package, allowing students to explore physics concepts through practical activities wherever they are.

Core Pillars

Visual Identity

I wanted the identity to feel educational without looking overly academic. The visual system combines a dark technical base with soft lavender and warm neutral tones to make the product feel both scientific and approachable.

Packaging Architecture

Designed the unboxing experience to be intuitive, with custom inserts that ensure fragile instruments are protected and easily identifiable.

Outcome & Learnings

STEM SET required me to think beyond the screen. Designing for a physical-digital hybrid product taught me how deeply hardware constraints influence software design.

The resulting brand has successfully bridged the gap between play and education, proving that complex subjects don't have to look boring to be taken seriously.
